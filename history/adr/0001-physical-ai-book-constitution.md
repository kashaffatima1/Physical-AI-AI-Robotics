# ADR-1: Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics Book Constitution

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2025-12-09

## Context

For the "Physical AI & Humanoid Robotics – A Hands-On Beginner to Intermediate Guide" book project, we need to establish foundational principles, constraints, success criteria, and governance structure before beginning content creation. This constitution will guide all future decisions about content, structure, and development approach.

## Decision

We have established a comprehensive constitution document that includes:

- Vision: Create an accessible, practical guide with hands-on projects and clear theoretical foundations
- Core Principles: 70% hands-on tutorials, 30% theory, progressive complexity, accessibility
- Success Criteria: Measurable learning outcomes and engagement metrics
- Constraints: Budget, time, technical limitations, and Docusaurus compatibility
- Stakeholders: Primary and secondary audiences, technology partners, and publishing team
- Brand Voice: Approachable, practical, inclusive, and action-oriented
- Content Structure: Docusaurus-friendly organization with progressive chapters
- Quality Standards: Code verification, troubleshooting sections, expert review

## Alternatives

- **No formal constitution**: Risk of inconsistent content direction and decision-making
- **Lightweight constitution**: Minimal guidance that might not cover all necessary aspects
- **Overly complex constitution**: Risk of creating a document too rigid to adapt during development

## Consequences

**Positive:**
- Clear direction for content creators and reviewers
- Consistent quality and tone throughout the book
- Measurable success criteria for project evaluation
- Defined stakeholder expectations and responsibilities

**Negative:**
- Initial time investment before content creation begins
- Risk of being too prescriptive and limiting creative approaches
- Need for maintenance if project requirements change
